## Limits of this verification

Earlier single-account live free/busy verification is documented in README. Live bilateral scheduling and Google writes are not claimed. The delivered calendar action is an `.ics` export requiring manual import.

Physical phone camera/keyboard/safe-area behavior remains untested. Actual QR image decoding and mobile/desktop browser layouts were exercised. Event attendance is user-reported or fictional demo data, and venues are participant proposals. The finite deterministic ranking vocabulary is intentionally conservative.

## Agent conversation UX iteration

The revised network separates For you, Agents and People. Agents groups exchanges by person, shows actual request/reply timestamps, highlights human decisions, and keeps meeting coordination inside the same conversation. Sharing and QR controls moved into a compact toolbar. The selected conversation persists in the URL.

New backend history records request, response, missing-context/permission pause, explicit owner answer and decline. Meeting history records proposal, availability permission, options, approvals/counterproposals, confirmation, failure and revocation. Retries do not duplicate entries. Historical field values and individual events are filtered again against current authorization. Legacy records do not invent missing response timestamps.
